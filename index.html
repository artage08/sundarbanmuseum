About the museum

A place where objects become stories.

Sundarban B C GOL Museum is a cultural and educational museum dedicated to preserving significant objects, interpreting regional and global heritage, and making history engaging for visitors of every age.

Our collection brings together archaeology, decorative arts, sculpture, painting, social history and archival material. Through exhibitions, digital stories, learning programmes and community activities, the museum helps connect the past with the questions and experiences of the present.

This homepage offers only a short introduction. The full history of the institution, its mission, collections, research work and curatorial approach is described on the dedicated About page.
